Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what data the VinylLens app for iPhone, iPad and Android collects, why we collect it, who we share it with and what choices you have. VinylLens is published by the developer named on its App Store and Google Play pages ("we," "our," or "us"), who is responsible for the data described here.

The Short Version

  • You don't need an account. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your record collection is stored on your device.
  • To identify a record, the app sends the photo you scan to our server, which uses an AI service to recognize it. We don't keep the photos.
  • We collect usage and crash data, tied only to a random identifier, to improve the app.
  • Payments are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Information We Collect

Record Photos and Label Text:

When you scan a record or choose a photo from your library, the app crops the image to the record and sends it to our server, together with the text the app has read from the label on your device and your currency. Our server passes the image to an AI provider to recognize the record and sends the result back to the app. When the app reads a barcode, only the barcode number is sent.

We do not store your photos on our server; they are discarded once the request is answered. The cover photos you keep in your collection stay on your device.

Collection Data:

Your records, collections, prices and app settings are stored only on your device (and in your device backup, if you use one). They are not uploaded to our servers. When you export your collection, the file is created on your device and shared only where you choose to send it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Usage and Diagnostic Data:

We use PostHog, an analytics service with servers in the European Union, to understand how the app is used and to find and fix bugs. The app sends events such as:

  • App opened, onboarding steps and screens viewed
  • Scans started and finished, including the artist and title of the identified record
  • Records saved and collections exported
  • Paywall views and the steps of a purchase
  • Errors, response times and crash reports

Each event includes the app version, device model, operating system version, language, time zone and a random identifier created when the app is installed. PostHog receives your IP address and uses it to determine an approximate location (country and city). The same random identifier is sent with recognition requests, so that server errors can be matched with app errors. It is not linked to your name or to any account.

Purchases:

Subscriptions are bought through the App Store or Google Play, and we never see your payment details. RevenueCat processes your purchase receipts, together with a random app user ID, so the app knows whether you have Premium. The purchase steps (for example, the plan chosen and whether the purchase was completed or cancelled) are also recorded in our analytics.

Network Data:

Like any internet service, our server and Cloudflare, which sits in front of it, process your IP address to deliver responses and to protect the service from abuse. Album cover images are loaded from the image server of the record database we use, which also receives your IP address when it delivers an image.

What We Don't Collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or contacts
  • Your precise (GPS) location
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA or Android Advertising ID). The app shows no ads and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How We Use Your Information

  • To recognize records and show album details and prices
  • To provide and restore your Premium subscription
  • To find and fix crashes, errors and slow responses
  • To understand which features are used and improve the app
  • To keep our service secure and prevent abuse

Third-Party Services

We share data only with the service providers that run the app for us:

  • Our server (hosted by OVHcloud) and Cloudflare - hosting and network for record recognition
  • Google (Gemini API) and, for older app versions, OpenAI - AI recognition of record photos. They process the images under their API terms and may keep requests for a limited time, for example to detect abuse.
  • PostHog (European Union) - analytics and crash reports
  • RevenueCat - subscription status and purchase receipts
  • Apple and Google - app distribution and payments. On Android, Google ML Kit reads barcodes and label text on your device and may send Google anonymous metrics about how these features perform.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, property or safety.

Legal Basis

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process your data because it is necessary to provide the service you ask for (record recognition and purchases), and on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the app working, secure and improving (analytics and crash reports).

International Transfers

Some of our providers, such as Google, OpenAI, RevenueCat and Cloudflare, may process data in the United States or other countries outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens, they rely on safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Data Retention

  • Photos are not stored by us. AI providers may keep them briefly under their own terms.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept only as long as needed to analyze and improve the app, and are then deleted.
  • Purchase records are kept as long as needed to provide your subscription and to meet legal obligations.
  • Data on your device stays there until you delete it or the app.

Data Security

All data the app sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). While we take steps to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing (including analytics), to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because we don't know your name and use only random identifiers, we usually can't link data to you on our own. Contact us and we will help; we may ask for details that let us find the data, such as the date of a scan or your purchase receipt. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Children's Privacy

VinylLens is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or our providers change. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.
